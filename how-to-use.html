How to Convert JPG to PDF - Easy Guide

Converting your JPG images to PDF is very easy with our tool. Just follow these 3 simple steps:

  1. Step 1: Upload Images: Go to our homepage JPG to PDF Tool and click on Upload button. Select all JPG images you want to convert. You can select multiple images at once.
  2. Step 2: Arrange Images: After uploading, you can drag and drop to arrange the order of images as you want them in the final PDF.
  3. Step 3: Convert & Download: Click on the Convert to PDF button. Within seconds, your PDF will be ready. Click Download to save it on your device.

This tool works on all devices - Mobile, Laptop, and PC. No installation is needed. It is the best solution for students who need to make assignments PDF or for office work.
